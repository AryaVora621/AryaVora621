### ⚡ About Me

> *"I design and build physical systems from first principles — mechanical CAD, custom PCB layout, embedded control firmware, and modern agentic software."*

- 🛠️ **Full-Stack Physical Engineering**: From scratch-built Cartesian 3D printers and multi-tool toolchangers to custom 4-legged quadruped robots, 6-axis arm carrier PCBs, and autonomous quadcopter flight controllers.

### 🔍 Engineering Deep Dives

<details>
<summary><b>🤖 FIRST Tech Challenge (Team 23786 MakEMinds) — Autonomous Control & Subsystems</b></summary>
<br/>

- **Autonomous Trajectory Generation**: Integrated **Pedro Pathing** utilizing Bézier curves defined by Bernstein polynomials and the de Casteljau algorithm for continuous curvature real-time obstacle avoidance.
- **Vision Localization**: Limelight 3A AprilTag detection paired with sub-pixel pose estimation to dynamically correct robot odometry drift during competition cycles.
- **Velocity Compensation**: Closed-loop PID velocity compensator tuning flywheel RPM relative to battery voltage sag and target range.
- **Physical Subsystems**: High-speed compliant Mecanum intake, motorized indexing transfer mechanism, and an angle-adjusting hood outtake with dual weighted flywheels.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>⚡ Gerald Rev 2 — Custom 6-Axis Robotic Arm Carrier PCB Architecture</b></summary>
<br/>

- **Compute & Connectivity**: Espressif ESP32-S3 microcontroller with dedicated hardware UART buses routed for serial bus servos.
- **High-Current Power Stage**: Integrated 12V 10A bus powering 6x STS3215 high-torque serial bus servos, supported by an onboard synchronous step-down buck converter.
- **Signal Integrity & Protection**: Star-grounding topology isolating logic and power planes, TVS diode reverse voltage protection, and comprehensive LC ripple filtering.
- **Design Rule Compliance**: Layout routed entirely in KiCad 8 with zero DRC clearance errors or unrouted nets.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🛠️ Multi-Material System (AIO_MMS) & Orochi Toolchanger</b></summary>
<br/>

- **AIO MMS (4-Filament Switcher)**: Parametrically modeled in Autodesk Fusion 360 and OpenSCAD with a motorized selector hub, high-torque feeder drives, and custom Klipper MMU macros.
- **Orochi Toolchanger**: CoreXY kinematic conversion on an Ender 5 Pro chassis using Klipper firmware, StealthChanger kinematic docking mounts, and custom toolhead wiring harnesses.

</details>

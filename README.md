# Hi, I'm Arya

I'm a junior (class of 2028) at John P. Stevens High School in Edison, NJ. I build robots and the electronics that run them: CAD, PCB layout, firmware, and the software on top.

I captain FTC Team 23786 (MakEMinds) and I'm on the board of FRC Team 2554 (The Warhawks).

## Robotics

**FTC 23786 MakEMinds.** I started the team and captain it. We won the NJ State Championship, won the NJ University Cup, and went to the FIRST World Championship in Houston, where our division ranked us 39th. On the robot I work on autonomous pathing (Pedro Pathing), Limelight vision, and flywheel velocity control.

**FRC 2554 Warhawks.** I handle sponsorships, strategy, and scouting for a team of 50+ people.

**Mentoring.** Over 500 hours across four FLL teams, plus 15 outreach events that reached about 2,700 students. I also raised $15,000+ in sponsorships and started a second FTC team, PulseDrive.

## Things I'm building

- [roboPet](https://github.com/AryaVora621/roboPet): a four-legged robot dog. A Pi Pico handles gait and inverse kinematics, and a Pi Zero 2W handles vision and speech.
- [Gerald](https://github.com/AryaVora621/gerald): a custom ESP32-S3 carrier board for a 6-axis robot arm running six STS3215 servos. Designed in KiCad.
- [Drone](https://github.com/AryaVora621/drone): a quadcopter flight controller and handheld transmitter on ESP32, talking over ESP-NOW.
- [Orochi](https://github.com/AryaVora621/orochi): an Ender 5 Pro turned into a CoreXY toolchanger running Klipper.
- [M.I.R.A.](https://github.com/AryaVora621/m.i.r.a): a room assistant that calibrates a projector to a camera with ArUco markers. Rust daemon, local voice.
- [Nana E-Book Reader](https://github.com/AryaVora621/nana-ebook-reader): an offline reading device for accessibility. Camera, OCR, and text-to-speech on a Pi Zero 2W.
- [notchTerm](https://github.com/AryaVora621/notchTerm): a macOS notch overlay that shows terminal and process status.
- [SmartInvest](https://github.com/AryaVora621/SmartInvest): a stock research dashboard with streaming AI analysis, built in Next.js.
